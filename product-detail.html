Shake Awake™ Technology

Shake Awake Technology is a standard feature on all current model Holosun optics and serves to extend battery life by deactivating the optic's LED while not in use. A highly sensitive motion sensor works to detect motion keeping the LED powered on during use. The slightest touch of the firearm instantly reactivates the LED so your optic is ready when needed.

Multiple Reticle System (MRS)

MRS-equipped optics allow you to determine the type of aiming point you want to use. Select a fast sight picture for shorter distances with a 32 MOA circle with 2 MOA dot, or deactivate the large ring leaving just the 2 MOA dot for an uncluttered sight picture and increased precision at range.
